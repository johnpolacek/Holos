# Holos Glossary

Canonical names for the framework's key terms, what each means, which variants are fine, and which to avoid. The site content stays the source of truth for positions (`Logic.tsx` for definitions and commitments); this page records naming. Entries marked **[F#]** have an open flag listed at the bottom.

Status: built 2026-09-29 from a full read of Overview, Logic, Predictions, citations, tables, and animation captions. Flags F1 to F10, F12, and F13 applied the same day; F11 and F14 (retitles) resolved after one-at-a-time review; F15 resolved the same day in a plain-language pass.

## Framework and notation

- **Holos**: the framework (Greek *holos*, "whole"). The framework is named for the relation it marks (D6), so "Holos (⊛)" as a title or logo is fine. Avoid: using "Holos" for the whole of reality; that is Omega.
- **The Holos relation**: `R = C ⊛ O`. Avoid: "Holos mapping", "Holos operator".
- **⊛, composition**: "possibility, then registration"; `C ⊛ O = O ∘ C`. An order of logic, not of time; nothing exotic in the glyph. Avoid: "operator", "recursive", "feedback loop", "C and O constrain each other".
- **R, lived reality**: the family of lived perspectives, one per observer per branch, each with the lit world it draws on. Structure that is also lived; a label for a difference, not a grade of reality. Avoid: "reality in the full sense", "realized world", "actualized reality".
- **C, Creation**: everything physics actually produces from the universe's state, every branch of the one quantum state. Fine: "the branches physics produces", "the structural layer", "possibility space" (the view from inside one branch). Avoid: "possibility structure" (a second name for possibility space), "every world the laws permit", "every possibility remains".
- **O, Observation**: registration from within, by an observer. Selects nothing, erases nothing, and in Holos without collapse changes nothing it registers. Avoid: calling the threshold crossing itself "Observation", "selection", "closure condition".
- **S**: an informational state of the universe at some level of description.

## Primitives

- **Information (D1)**: differentiation between possible states of a system.
- **Relation (D2)**: a constraint linking informational states; structure is stable patterns of relation.
- **Consciousness (D4)**: hosting an integrated perspective: being an observer. The everyday word for what the framework calls observerhood and experience. Avoid: "the capacity" to host one.
- **Experience**: what is lived; the one fact Holos starts from. **Fundamental** means underived, never "everywhere" or "free of physics".

## Where experience is (D7)

- **Lived**: where experience occurs, inside observers and nowhere else.
- **Lit**: binary; a region in the causal past of at least one observer in its branch. "An observer lights its past."
- **Unlit**: structure outside every observer's causal past (observer-free branches, regions beyond every observer's horizon). Real as pattern, never lived.
- **Witnessing**: graded; how much of the lit region an observer's experience is actually about.
- **Presence**: being lived; the noun for what "lived" describes (D7). Where there is no observer, there is structure without presence. For the adjective write "lived", not "present as experience" or "experientially present".
- **Walled off**: apertures cut off from one another. Retired: "sealed", "sealing".
- **Structural facts / registered facts** (Commitment 3): structural facts are absolute (laws, branches and weights, whether a system meets the threshold); registered facts are indexed to observers.
- **Register vs record**: only observers register. Detectors, instruments, and records record.

## Observers

- **Observer**: a system that meets the four observer requirements, with integration past the twilight at a local maximum. Fine in plain prose: "point of view", "perspective" (what an observer hosts), "conscious system" (especially when describing rival views), "someone home".
- **Aperture**: the same system seen from Omega's side, "an opening through which the whole registers itself". Use it in Omega and monist-reading passages; use "observer" in definitions, requirements, and tests. "Local aperture" is fine on first use.
- **Observerhood**: the status of being an observer.
- **Observer requirements**: integration (which includes feedback), differentiation, temporal cohesion, aboutness. Avoid: "observer criteria", "these criteria"; never list recursion or causal autonomy separately.
- **Aboutness**: the system's integrated states carry a model of something beyond it. Structural, not historical.
- **Maximality condition** (listed as "Exclusion"): an aperture forms only where integration reaches a local maximum. Avoid: "maximality rule".
- **Integration (Φ)**: how far a system's parts constrain one another as one whole; borrowed from IIT as a measure only. Note: the Integration Hypothesis uses "integration" in a civilizational sense; the site already disclaims this in Aliens and needs no change.

## The threshold

- **The threshold, Φ_c**; long form "the integration threshold". Avoid: "observer threshold", "critical level", and "critical point" or "critical transition" for the crossing ("critical point" stays correct for waking cortex's operating point).
- **Twilight**: the narrow stretch around Φ_c with no exact fact of the matter.
- **The threshold in three claims**: (1) edge, (2) integration (the core claim, Test A), (3) transition.
- **Transition hypothesis**: claim 3; the crossing is a genuine transition, sudden or continuous. Tested by Test B.

## The whole

- **Omega**: the whole of reality. Physically the universal quantum state; experientially the one experiencer. Fine: "the totality", "the whole", "the one experiencer", "the one subject", "the universal quantum state" (or "the universe's complete quantum state"). Avoid: "Omega Point" except when naming Teilhard or Tipler; "Ω" in prose; "limit", "endpoint", "horizon"; "ground of record agreement"; "fundamental posit"; "one process".
- **The two additions (to physics)**: the threshold (Axiom 3) and Omega (Axiom 5). Avoid: "ingredients", "fundamental posits".
- **Monist reading**: the reading that the whole is the one experiencer (Axiom 5). Keep it distinct from two-sided monism.
- **Open individualism**: the established name (Kolak) for the view that there is one person and every one of us is it.
- **Two-sided monism with a threshold**: Axiom 4's kind of view; neither physicalism nor dualism.

## Physics side

- **No-collapse reading**: Axiom 2 read as unitary evolution with branching. Avoid: "no-collapse picture".
- **Holos without collapse**: the version this site defends and bets on. Fine: "the version defended here".
- **Holos with collapse**: the version declared in advance for a lost bet.
- **Apparent collapse**: a record's definiteness within a branch; nothing an observer adds.
- **Objective collapse / consciousness collapse**: the two rival collapse views (Diósi-Penrose style; Chalmers and McQueen style).
- **Branch**: one complete way the universe can go.
- **Born weights**: structural facts within C; for an observer, self-locating odds. "Quantum weights" is fine in plain-language passages.
- **Standing bet**: consciousness adds no new physics.

## Method and structure

- **Closure** (Proposition IV): each higher description holds as a closed whole what the level below sees as endless or unfolding; last is Omega, the description with nothing outside it. Fine: "higher description", "one level up". Avoid: "ladder of descriptions", "rung", "climb", "at the top" (they make it sound like somewhere to go); "Dimensional Resolution" (the old title).
- **The core**: Axioms 1, 3, 4, and 5. Never "all five".
- **Claims**: the Logic section (`#minimal-core`, nav "Claims") that inventories every claim by status, including ones marked "not core". Avoid: "Minimal Core", "Core", "Overview" (the home page), "Declaration" ("declared in advance" names the collapse fallback).
- **Commitments 1 to 3**, **Test A**, **Test B**, **Check C**: as titled on Predictions.
- **Companion ideas**: the Integration Hypothesis and the Teeming Dark.

## Companion ideas and speculation

- **Integration Hypothesis**: mature civilizations turn compact, efficient, and quiet in light.
- **Going Quiet**: the progressive reduction in a civilization's external signatures. Retired: "Visibility Collapse" (collapse is reserved for quantum collapse and its ordinary senses).
- **Teeming Dark**: the possibility that silence and abundant life coexist.
- **Dark Node**: ordinary matter that has stopped shining; never cosmological dark matter.
- **Holosian Scale** (capitalized, like the Kardashev Scale; H0 to H5), **mesostructures**, **Holocore**, **Computronium Kernel**, **Chrono Vault**, **Sentinel Probes**.
