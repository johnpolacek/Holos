# Figure List

Status: draft for John's approval, 2026-09-29. Scope: the site keeps its layout and text; every animation and illustration is replaced by an engraved 3D figure, and new figures are added wherever they help ("more the better"). Each figure auto plays when it scrolls into view, steps through 2 to 6 stages with a short caption per stage, and has pause, step, and replay controls. The PDF gets a static engraved version.

Full detail (every stage caption, exact paragraph, and line numbers) lives in [figures-logic.md](figures-logic.md) and [figures-predictions.md](figures-predictions.md). Overview figures draw on [storyboard.md](storyboard.md).

**How to review:** mark each ID keep, cut, or change. Figures that appear on more than one page are built once and reused; they are listed where they first appear and cross-referenced after that.

## Rules every figure follows

- The **iris** is the only mark for an observer. Nothing below Φ_c gets one: no dish culture, photon "friend", detector, gate grid, or AI proposal.
- **Arrows point into observers,** never out. Observation never changes, selects, or lights anything.
- **Lit regions are engraved with everything else,** never switched on after an observer appears.
- **Irises sit partway along branches,** never at forks.
- **Status cartouche** on every figure, matching the claims box: physics, a side taken, an addition, a hypothesis, a companion idea, a thought experiment, speculation. It changes mid-figure when the status changes.
- **Dashed lines mean not real or not yet known:** possible outcomes, proposed designs, hypothetical curves. Solid means established physics or actual findings.
- **Two kinds of uncertainty, two looks:** scatter for instrument error, hatching for the twilight.
- **Omega** has no center, no glow, no rays, and is never the top or end of anything.
- Instruments **record**; only observers **register**. Captions use glossary terms and no em dashes.
