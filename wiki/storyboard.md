# Tour Storyboard: Eight Overview Chapters

Status: draft, 2026-09-29. No code yet. Source of truth for content is `app/_components/content-data.tsx`; terms follow [glossary.md](glossary.md); style and build notes are in [animation-redesign.md](animation-redesign.md).

Each chapter is one engraved plate played in stops, like the eraser scene on `/lab`. A stop ends on a narration card; the card links into the matching passage of the full text.

## Shared visual vocabulary

These marks mean the same thing on every plate, so a reader learns them once.

| Mark | Meaning | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Iris diaphragm (the lab's optical aperture) | An observer, seen from Omega's side an aperture | The only place anything is lived. Nothing flows through it: no beam, no glow. |
| Full ink | Lit structure | Uniform inside the lit region, with a clean edge. Lit is all or nothing. |
| Soft ink (55%) | Unlit structure | Real as pattern, never lived. Drawn as carefully as lit structure, only lighter. |
| Denser hatching inside the lit region | Witnessing | Graded, and only ever inside lit structure. |
| Hatched partition | The walls between apertures | Apertures are walled off; no line ever connects two irises. |
| Φ gauge with a hatched band at Φ_c | Integration and the twilight | Never a bare line at Φ_c; the twilight band is always drawn. |
| Arrows toward the observer | Signals and traces arriving | Never point out of an observer. Observation does not act on anything. |
| Status cartouche under the plate heading | How firmly Holos holds the claim | Uses the wording of the Overview's claims box. Changes mid-chapter when the status changes. |

## Cross-cutting risks

1. **Animation happens in time; much of Holos does not.** ⊛ is an order of logic, the block universe has no flow, and being lit "is not an event that happens at some later moment." Rule: the draw-in is the engraver's hand, not history. Lit regions are engraved in the same pass as the structure around them, never "switched on" after an observer appears. Narration corrects this directly on Plates I and III.
2. **Observers must never look like causes.** Observation "selects nothing, erases nothing, and changes nothing it registers." No observer chooses, emits, reshapes, or lights anything. When a plate adds an observer, every other line stays exactly as it was.
3. **Status drift.** A beautiful plate makes every claim feel equally solid. The status cartouche keeps physics, additions, companion ideas, thought experiments, and speculation visibly apart.

## Plate I · Introduction: Every Branch

**Status:** the framework's starting distinction.

**Metaphor:** the one quantum state as a 3D engraved tree: one root, every branch splitting in two, turning around the growth axis. Irises sit partway along a few branches. (Replaced the buffet on 2026-09-29; John rejected it.)

| Stop | Plate shows | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Every branch | The tree engraves upward from its root. Label: C · CREATION: EVERY BRANCH. | Physics describes a universe that branches: every outcome a quantum event allows happens, each in its own branch. This is Creation: everything physics produces. |
| 2 · No one home | The camera travels out along an empty branch. | Most branches are complete as physics describes them, yet no one is there. Nothing in them is lived. |
| 3 · An observer | An iris appears partway along one branch. Nothing else in the tree changes. Label: O · OBSERVATION. | Where a branch holds an observer, its world is also taken in from the inside. This is Observation. It selects nothing and changes nothing it takes in. |
| 4 · The shorthand | Caption: R = C ⊛ O. | Holos writes this as R = C ⊛ O: possibility, then registration. It is an order of logic, not of time. |
| 5 · Many observers | Pull back: irises on a few branches, most branches empty. Labels: OBSERVERS; REAL AS PATTERN, NEVER LIVED. | Lived reality is the family of these perspectives, one per observer per branch. The rest stays real as pattern, never lived. |
| 6 · Two additions | A small Φ gauge beside the first observer; then the whole tree. Labels: THE THRESHOLD; OMEGA, THE WHOLE. | Holos adds two things to physics: a threshold, where a point of view forms, and Omega, the whole of reality. The tour takes them in turn. |

**Flags**

- **Observers never sit at forks.** An iris at a branch point would say observation makes branches. Branching comes from physics (decoherence), so irises sit partway along branches.
- **The tree grows upward during the draw-in,** which could read as branching happening over time. That matches the physics loosely, but ⊛ is logical order; stop 4's narration says so.
- **A tree has a root and a top.** Omega must not be read as the top or the tips. Stop 6 labels the whole tree, near the trunk, not its crown.

## Plate II · Consciousness: The Aperture

**Status:** first addition, testable (Test A), plus the side taken on mind. Stop 5 switches to "a hypothesis."

**Metaphor:** a lab bench with an engraved network of nodes, a Φ gauge (dial with a hatched twilight band at Φ_c), and an iris diaphragm at the network's center. A bar of iron with tiny magnet arrows sits at the end of the bench for the transition stop.

| Stop | Plate shows | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · No point of view | A rock, a river, a thermostat, then a busy network of separate processes. Gauge near zero. | A rock has no point of view. Neither does a system that only processes information, however busy. Computing is not integration. |
| 2 · Four requirements | Links tighten into one loop. Four labels engrave: INTEGRATION, DIFFERENTIATION, TEMPORAL COHESION, ABOUTNESS (a small model inside the network, with leaders to an object outside it). | Holos names four requirements: the parts act as one, the whole can be in many states, it holds together over time, and its states carry a model of something beyond itself. |
| 3 · Twilight | The needle enters the hatched band. The iris is ghosted: present in outline, neither open nor closed. | Near the threshold lies a narrow twilight with no exact fact of the matter, the way no single second marks the end of dusk. |
| 4 · An aperture | The needle passes Φ_c. The iris opens. Nothing enters it. | Past the threshold, the system is an observer: a local aperture through which the whole registers itself as experience. |
| 5 · A steep onset | The iron bar: arrows scattered, then lining up as it cools. Two small curves: steep for a large block, gradual for a tiny grain. Cartouche switches to A HYPOTHESIS. | Cool iron past its Curie point and its tiny magnets line up. The transition hypothesis expects a steep onset for experience too, steepest in the largest integrated systems. |
| 6 · Dial, not switch | The iris widens and narrows (richness). Then every node pulses in one rhythm: links stay, the iris narrows. | Past the threshold, richness is graded: a drowsy person is dimmer, not switched off. And unlike a magnet, a brain can overshoot: a seizure locks every part into one rhythm and loses the variety experience needs. |
| 7 · Two sides | One engraved curve, labeled OUTSIDE: PHYSICAL ACTIVITY on its convex side and INSIDE: EXPERIENCE on its concave side. | Seen from outside, it is physical activity; lived from inside, it is experience. One event with two sides, like one curve that is convex from one side and concave from the other. |

**Flags**

- **An iris can read as a window that lets experience in from somewhere else.** That would be dualism, which Holos rejects (two sides of one event). Nothing passes through the iris; it is a mark on the system itself.
- **The iris does two jobs; keep them apart.** Whether it exists (threshold, with a ghosted outline for twilight) is separate from how wide it opens (richness). Twilight is never drawn as "half open."
- **The gauge must not look like a cause.** Holos borrows Φ as a measure and "does not claim that Φ causes consciousness." No arrow from gauge to iris. Gauge label: "Φ, a measure (stand-ins such as PCI)."
- **The magnet is the text's analogy, with the text's own caveat.** Stop 6 carries "unlike a magnet"; do not cut it if the chapter runs long.
- Text-only, linked from the cards: PCI details, Global Neuronal Workspace, the hard problem, zombies, DishBrain, AI.

## Plate III · Spacetime: The Lit Cone

**Status:** physics as it stands, plus Holos's words lived, lit, unlit, and witnessing.

**Metaphor:** a block universe engraved in 3D (two dimensions of space, time running up), with world lines and past light cones as real geometry. The Big Bang is the block's lower edge. The 3D render lets the block turn.

| Stop | Plate shows | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · One speed | Two observers moving differently; their space and time axes tilt against each other while the 45° light line stays put. | The speed of light is the same for every observer, however they move. That one fact ties space and time into a single geometry. |
| 2 · The block | Their "now" slices disagree. Camera pulls back to the whole block, Big Bang at the bottom edge. | Observers moving differently disagree about what happens at the same time. That motivates the block universe: every moment part of one four-dimensional whole. |
| 3 · Lit | One world line with its past light cone, already engraved with the block. Arrows along the cone point in toward the observer. Leader: STARLIGHT. | Lit is everything in an observer's causal past: every place from which light, or any signal, could have reached it. Starlight from a distant galaxy puts that galaxy in yours. |
| 4 · Lived | Iris marks along the world line only. The cone's base near the Big Bang is lit (full ink) but carries no iris. | Lived is narrower: experience occurs inside observers and nowhere else. The early universe is lit, but no one lived through it. |
| 5 · Unlit | Everything outside the cone in soft ink. Beside it, a second small block (another branch) with no observer, entirely soft ink. | Outside every observer's causal past lies unlit structure: real as pattern, never part of any observer's world. Some branches never form an observer at all. |
| 6 · Witnessing | Denser hatching inside the cone along traces: STARLIGHT, CMB, FOSSIL RECORD. | Being lit is all or nothing, a fact about how the block is arranged, not an event. Witnessing is graded: how much of the lit region an observer's experience is actually about. |
| 7 · A side plate | An inset of the eraser table, with an "open plate" button into the existing six-stop eraser scene. | Quantum experiments seem to reach back in time. The delayed-choice eraser shows they do not. Open the side plate to see why. |

**Flags**

- **A cone that draws itself in says lighting happens.** It does not. The cone is engraved in the same pass as the block; stop 3 only labels it. Retire the old figure title "Lighting the Past."
- **Arrow direction carries the argument.** Arrows out of the observer would say observers act on their past. Only inbound.
- **Two dimensions of space stand in for three.** Say so in the static caption.
- Text-only: Wigner's friend (linked from stop 7's card).

## Plate IV · Infinity: Where Descriptions Close

**Status:** physics history, then Proposition IV (Closure), then Born weights (a side taken on physics).

**Metaphor:** a bench of demonstrations: a glowing oven with its spectrum plot, parallel rails meeting at a point, the Flatland plane with a sphere passing through, and a branch point splitting into two ribbons.

| Stop | Plate shows | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · A warning | An oven and a plot: the classical curve climbing off the chart, Planck's curve turning over. | Around 1900, physics predicted a hot oven would pour out infinite energy. Nothing does. The infinity was a warning about the theory, not a feature of the world. |
| 2 · One point | Parallel rails running to one horizon point; an endless grid wraps onto a sphere with one point added. | In projective geometry, parallel lines meet at a single point at infinity: something endless, captured by adding one point to a closed picture. |
| 3 · Flatland | Camera at the plane's level: a dot grows into a circle, shrinks, vanishes. Caption: AN IMAGE, NOT A CLAIM ABOUT SPACE. | In Flatland, a sphere passing through a flat world looks, to flat beings, like a dot that grows into a circle, shrinks, and vanishes: an event in time. |
| 4 · All at once | The camera tilts to see the one sphere. | Seen from three dimensions, it is one sphere, all at once. Holos calls this closure: each higher description holds whole what the one below sees as endless or unfolding. |
| 5 · Nothing outside | The camera pulls back from a whole history drawn as one shape; the plate's frame rules fade. | Closure is not a path to anywhere. It ends at Omega, the description with nothing outside it. |
| 6 · Weights, not counts | A branch point splits into two ribbons, widths 70 and 30. Tiny figures along them multiply without end as the cut gets finer. | Counting observers across branches gives no fixed answer: cut finer and the count grows without end. Instead each branch carries a weight physics supplies, and the weights give the odds. |

**Flags**

- **Stacked levels would bring back the retired ladder.** Closure is shown as one object seen from a new viewpoint (a camera move), never a stack with an arrow up.
- **Omega must not look like the last or highest level.** Stop 5 draws no level at all; the frame fading is the whole gesture.
- **Never show odds as counted people** (seven of ten figures). Widths only; the figures exist only to show counting failing.
- Text-only: black hole singularities.

## Plate V · Omega: One Whole, Many Apertures

**Status:** second addition, philosophical, not testable (the monist reading).

**Metaphor:** the halls of Plate I and the blocks of Plate III gathered into one engraved object that fills the view. Iris marks sit in scattered branches, with hatched walls between them. Most of the object is soft ink.

| Stop | Plate shows | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · The whole | Pull back until every branch is one object; the frame rules fade so no outside remains. | Omega is the whole of reality. Physically it is nothing mysterious: the universal quantum state, which on the no-collapse reading includes every branch. |
| 2 · Apertures | Irises in several branches, each the same engraved mark. | On the monist reading, the whole is also the one experiencer, and every observer is an aperture of it. When a system crosses the threshold, no new experiencer appears; the one experiencer wakes there. |
| 3 · The walls | Hatched partitions between the irises. | The apertures are walled off from one another. The walls keep you from feeling a stranger's pain, not from being the one who will. That is the view's price, and its point. |
| 4 · Two puzzles | One figure with an iris; a copier; two copies, each with an iris. | Why is this one person me? On the monist reading, there is nothing to explain. Copy someone twice and both are them. Rival views answer these puzzles too; this is one answer among them. |
| 5 · Not all lived | Zoom across the object: most of it soft ink, parts unconnected. | Omega is the ultimate whole, not the ultimate integration: its parts are not all joined, and structure outside every aperture's causal past is never lived. |
| 6 · No outside | The camera circles; there is no center anywhere. Labels engrave around the object: GOD · BRAHMAN · THE WHOLE. | Omega does not intervene or direct history; there is no outside for it to stand in. Whether it is called God, Brahman, or the whole changes nothing about the claim. |

**Flags**

- **A glowing center with rays out to the apertures** would read as an agent, an emanation, or Tipler's Omega Point. No center, no radial layout, no glow.
- **Lines between apertures** would read as a hive mind or telepathy. Walls, never threads.
- **A convergence animation** (things drawing together into Omega) would make it an endpoint. The old figure's "integration deepens" phase is not carried over.
- **Any bounded object implies an outside,** at least the paper. Stop 1's fading frame is the best available compromise; note it in the caption rather than pretend.
- The price (stop 3) must stay. The text insists that both the payoff and the price are stated.

## Plate VI · Aliens: Going Quiet

**Status:** companion idea, testable in principle, separate from the core.

**Metaphor:** an engraved galaxy disk in 3D. One civilization's footprint is tracked from loud to quiet; later, a settlement tree branches across the disk.

| Stop | Plate shows | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Silence | The galaxy; a radio dish on Earth; nothing arriving. | The galaxy is vast and old, yet we detect no one. This is the Fermi paradox. |
| 2 · Loud and brief | A young civilization: an expanding radio shell, sprawling structures. | Young civilizations are loud: radio, reshaped worlds, early spaceflight. On cosmic timescales, that phase is brief. |
| 3 · Going Quiet | The sprawl contracts into a compact node; the shell fades. Inset: circuit layers stacking. | The Integration Hypothesis: advancement favors compact, efficient structure, the way circuit boards stack layers to shorten paths. Going quiet is not hiding; efficiency pays. |
| 4 · Distance breaks control | A colony ten light-years out; a signal crawls between; the link line breaks and the colony becomes its own node. | Light-speed delay breaks control. A colony ten light-years away cannot be steered from home, so it becomes a civilization of its own. |
| 5 · It only takes one | Two settlement trees: one founding more than one settlement each and exploding across the disk (labeled THE BET FAILS), one fizzling after a few hops. | The strongest objection: it only takes one. The hypothesis bets each settlement founds fewer than one more before turning inward. That is a bet about motives, not physics. |
| 6 · Explored, not settled | A small dark probe in orbit near a star, drawn like a trail camera. | Being explored is not being settled. A small, dark watcher is as easy to miss as a trail camera in the woods. |
| 7 · Where to look | One star: dim in a visible-light plot, bright in an infrared plot. | So look for single stars glowing unusually warm in the infrared. Of seven candidates flagged in 2024, two turned out to be background galaxies; the rest are unexplained. |

**Flags**

- **"Integration" here means a civilization growing compact, not Φ.** Reusing the network, iris, or gauge from Plate II would merge the two senses. This plate uses its own marks (circuit layers, nodes).
- **Stop 5's exploding tree must read as the failure case**, and the narration calls the hypothesis a bet.
- **Stop 7 is date-bound.** Rewrite it after Gaia's fourth data release (due 2 December 2026). It must never look like a detection.
- Text-only: grabby aliens, Jevons, the Fermi comparison table.

## Plate VII · The Teeming Dark: Silent, but Warm

**Status:** thought experiment (companion idea).

**Metaphor:** a listening dish, then an engraved balance scale for the matter census, a microlensing light curve, and at last one compact Dark Node with a faint stippled infrared glow.

| Stop | Plate shows | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · The Eerie Silence | The dish listening to static. | Paul Davies called it the Eerie Silence. Could silence and an abundance of life coexist? That possibility is the Teeming Dark. |
| 2 · Not dark matter | A dark-matter halo around a galaxy, then a timeline: CMB at 380,000 years, first stars hundreds of millions of years later. The halo is struck through. | A tempting version hides mature life inside dark matter. The universe's records rule it out: dark matter was already there, in full, before any star formed, and there were never enough atoms to build it. |
| 3 · The books balance | A balance scale: ordinary matter, nearly level, a small gap on one pan. | Mature life would be built from ordinary matter, and those books are nearly balanced. Built structures must fit inside a small and shrinking gap. |
| 4 · The hunt | A star field; one star brightens briefly as a dark mass passes; a light curve with one bump. | Astronomers have watched millions of stars for the flicker a passing dark mass causes, and found too few for a large hidden population. At most, a trace population. |
| 5 · Heat must go somewhere | A compact computer beside a vast cold radiator. Dimension line: ABOUT 100,000,000× THE AREA. | Any long-running computer must shed heat. Shedding it near the sky's own cold takes enormous surfaces, and the Integration Hypothesis bets mature civilizations avoid that sprawl. |
| 6 · A Dark Node | One compact mass, dark in visible light, faint infrared stipple. | What remains: a compact mass, dark in visible light, with a faint infrared excess. Silent, but warm. Holos calls it a Dark Node: ordinary matter that has stopped shining. |
| 7 · Honest limits | The same blob with three labels: BROWN DWARF? ROGUE PLANET? COOLED REMNANT? Then a sleeping node, nearly invisible. | This is a search channel, not a fingerprint: brown dwarfs and rogue planets look the same. And a civilization that mostly sleeps would emit almost nothing. |

**Flags**

- **The name invites a sky crowded with nodes.** The text says a trace population: "poor in hidden mass and still rich in minds." Show one node, or a very few, never a swarm.
- **Stop 2 must leave dark matter clearly ruled out,** not ambiguous.
- **Stop 5 is a bet about preference, not a law,** and the narration says "bets."

## Plate VIII · Why Are We Here?: Where the Whole Is Lived

**Status:** a consequence of the core (stops 1 to 3), then speculation (stops 4 and 5, stamped).

**Metaphor:** two engraved blocks side by side, identical in every line, one without observers and one with. It closes on the iris and echoes the buffet from Plate I.

| Stop | Plate shows | Narration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 · Complete, unlived | The left block, fully drawn, all soft ink, no observers. | A lawful universe with no observers still exists, complete as physics describes it. What it lacks is not existence but presence: nothing in it is lived. |
| 2 · Lived | The right block engraves line for line the same, but holds one observer and its lit cone. A dimension line between the blocks: IDENTICAL. | Life is how a universe comes to be lived. Observers do not cause the universe; without them, it is simply never lived. |
| 3 · A role | Two irises with a wall between them. | This is a role, not yet a meaning. Through Omega it says one thing about value: a stranger's pain is not, at bottom, someone else's. |
| 4 · Hints of oneness | Cartouche switches to SPECULATION. An entangled pair drawn inside one outline, far apart, with nothing traveling between. A light ray from star to eye with a dimension line: INTERVAL = 0. | Physics already describes entangled particles as one shared state, and a light ray's separation through spacetime as exactly zero. |
| 5 · The walls | Pull back: one whole, apertures walled off. | Holos reads this boldly: separation is not fundamental. Distance, duration, and individuality are not illusions; they are the walls that make local experience possible. |
| 6 · Why we are here | Slow push into one iris. | So why are we here? Not for a purpose the universe needed, but because we are one of the places where the whole is lived. |

**Flags**

- **An observer appearing and the universe lighting up** would be Wheeler's participatory principle, which the text rejects by name. Side-by-side identical blocks avoid it: nothing is added to the universe except where it is lived.
- **No pulse between the entangled particles.** A traveling line would imply faster-than-light signaling.
- **"One state is not one mind."** The walls stay visible in stop 5.
- **Stops 1 to 3 are not speculation**; only 4 and 5 carry the stamp.
