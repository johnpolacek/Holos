# Writing Principles

The principles behind John's editing passes. Apply them site-wide. The [glossary](glossary.md) governs term names, and `.cursor/rules/holos-guardrails.mdc` governs what may be claimed. Consolidated 2026-09-30 from 22 rules gathered in the Intro and Consciousness passes. Examples come from those edits.

## Sentences

1. **Terse and plain.** Short sentences in ordinary words. Mostly commas and periods. No em dashes, no semicolons, few colons. No filler, no excess explanation, no unnecessary headings, no insider jargon ("nothing it is like to be", "registers itself").
2. **Make the claim, and keep it honest.** Say the strong thing plainly ("Reality requires a witness"), not a balanced summary. Don't overclaim in the frame ("built on two ideas", not "comprised of" them). Don't praise our own ideas ("bold"). Keep what makes a claim credible: "it can fail", "should" on an untested prediction, the reason a fallback version exists.
3. **Open on the thing, end on the hardest line.** No throat-clearing lead-ins ("Holos adds two things to physics", "If Holos is correct, three things follow"). Close each paragraph on its strongest sentence, not on a qualification.

## Paragraphs

4. **One idea at a time.** One new idea per sentence, and few per paragraph. Introduce a term before stacking the next on it (aperture before Omega). Cut the sentence the next one implies. Two examples, not three.
5. **Picture first.** Show the thing itself, not an analogy that needs unpacking (a universe with no one in it, not a buffet). Choose examples that make one point without raising a side question (locked-in syndrome, not a dream). Set up contrast with parallel sentences ("It says how... It says nothing about...").
6. **Define by what is different.** Never define a term by itself ("joined tightly enough to form one point of view"). Say what is structurally different ("how fully a system's parts act as one"). Name the key word when the idea appears (structure vs reality). At first contact use the plainest word ("experienced"). Keep the defined term ("lived") for definitions and the formal pages.

## Pages

7. **The Overview makes the case. Depth lives elsewhere.** Comparisons with rival theories, measurement detail, named theories and acronyms, and defenses against objections go to Logic or Predictions, with a link. Merge paragraphs that do the same job. Cut summaries that repeat an earlier paragraph ("Holos is a middle position").
8. **Consistent forms.** Links read "(see X)", naming the destination. A set of parallel defined terms goes in small caps, one per line, behind a left rule.

## Holos vocabulary

9. **"Real" means lived, and experience changes nothing.** Reserve *reality* and *real* for lived reality (R). What physics produces is *structure*. Unlit structure *exists* as pattern but is not called real. Never let wording make experience act on the world ("to become realized"). Structure does not change when it is lived.
