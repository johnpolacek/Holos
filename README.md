# Holos: An Interpretive Framework for Understanding Reality, Bounded by Physics

**[whatisholos.vercel.app](https://whatisholos.vercel.app)**

We live in a universe described with extraordinary precision, yet filled with mystery. Physics tells us how matter moves, how spacetime bends, and how probabilities evolve, but *what does it mean to be real?*

Holos is an interpretive framework for understanding the nature of reality. It adds no new physical laws; its two additions, a threshold for what counts as an observer and a reading of the whole of reality as one experiencer, sit on top of established physics. It offers an explanation of how the universe described by physics relates to the universe we experience.

At its core, Holos expresses this idea as a simple relation: **R = C ⊛ O** (lived reality is Creation, then Observation): on the no-collapse reading Holos defends, physics produces every branch of the universe, and each branch with an observer is lived from within.

What follows explores this idea, from consciousness and the meaning of life to spacetime, infinity, and the whole, proposing answers to some of the most fundamental questions we can ask.
